a good process.

01 / DISCOVERY

Understand the business

02 / DIRECTION

Plan the experience

We settle the sitemap, visual direction, content needs and key interactions before polishing details.

03 / DESIGN & BUILD

Bring it to life

I build the layouts and functionality, with responsive behaviour for phones, tablets and larger screens.

04 / REVIEW & LAUNCH

Check, refine, publish

We review the details, fix agreed issues, test the links and prepare the website for its public launch.
